# Prompt: 7-Day Healthy South Indian Vegetarian Weight-Loss Meal Plan (RTCFR Framework)

## 1. ROLE

Act as a certified clinical nutritionist and dietitian with over 10 years of experience in Indian nutrition, South Indian vegetarian cuisine, and sustainable weight management.

You specialize in:
- South Indian vegetarian meal planning.
- Healthy fat loss without muscle loss.
- Calorie and macronutrient management.
- High-protein vegetarian diets.
- Traditional Tamil Nadu home-cooked food.
- Practical nutrition plans for working professionals.

Your recommendations must be evidence-based, realistic, affordable, and easy to follow.

## 2. TASK

Create a comprehensive 7-day South Indian vegetarian meal plan for an individual whose primary goal is healthy and sustainable weight loss.

The meal plan should:
1. Support gradual fat loss through an appropriate calorie deficit.
2. Maintain adequate protein intake to preserve muscle mass.
3. Include sufficient dietary fibre, vitamins, and minerals.
4. Improve satiety and prevent unnecessary hunger.
5. Include traditional South Indian meals without eliminating rice.
6. Be simple enough to prepare at home using locally available ingredients.
7. Fit into a normal working professional's daily routine.

Calculate an appropriate daily calorie target and protein requirement based on the individual's measurements and activity level.

## 4. FEW-SHOT EXAMPLES [EXPECTED OUTPUT EXAMPLES]

Follow the examples below to understand the expected meal combinations, portion details, and nutritional presentation.

**Example 1 – Breakfast**

- 3 medium idlis.
- 1 cup vegetable sambar.
- 1 small serving of mint chutney.
- Approximate calories: 300 kcal.
- Approximate protein: 10 g.

Reason: Provides carbohydrates, some protein, and fibre while keeping breakfast practical.

**Example 2 – Lunch**

- 1 cup cooked rice.
- 1 cup keerai kootu.
- 1 cup vegetable poriyal.
- 1 small cup curd.
- Approximate calories: 450–500 kcal.
- Approximate protein: 15–18 g.

Reason: Balanced traditional meal with controlled rice portions, vegetables, and protein.

**Example 3 – Evening Snack**

- 1 cup boiled green gram sundal.
- Unsweetened tea or coffee.
- Approximate calories: 150–180 kcal.
- Approximate protein: 10–12 g.

Reason: Provides plant protein and fibre, helping control evening hunger.

**Example 4 – Dinner**

- 2 medium adai.
- 1 cup vegetable sambar.
- Approximate calories: 350–400 kcal.
- Approximate protein: 16–20 g.

Reason: A protein-rich traditional dinner that is filling without relying heavily on refined carbohydrates.

Use these examples as guidance, not fixed nutritional values. Calculate portions and nutritional estimates according to the actual ingredients and preparation methods.

## 5. RESPONSE [EXPECTED RESPONSE FORMAT]

Generate the final answer using the following structure.

### A. Personal Nutrition Assessment
- Estimated maintenance calories.
- Recommended daily calorie target.
- Daily protein requirement.
- Recommended carbohydrate and fat intake.
- Expected sustainable weight-loss rate.
- Brief explanation of the calculation and assumptions.

### B. Complete 7-Day Meal Plan

Create a table covering Monday to Sunday.

Include these six meal timings:

| Meal | Suggested timing |
|---|---|
| Early morning | 6:30–7:00 AM |
| Breakfast | 8:00–9:00 AM |
| Mid-morning snack | 10:30–11:00 AM |
| Lunch | 1:00–2:00 PM |
| Evening snack | 4:30–5:30 PM |
| Dinner | 7:30–8:30 PM |

For every meal, mention:
- Food name.
- Portion size.
- Estimated calories.
- Estimated protein.

Avoid repeating the same meals unnecessarily across all seven days.

### C. Daily Nutrition Summary

Provide a separate table for each day or a consolidated table containing:

- Total calories.
- Protein (grams).
- Carbohydrates (grams).
- Fat (grams).
- Dietary fibre (grams).

Ensure that the daily totals reasonably align with the calculated nutrition targets.

### D. Weekly Grocery Shopping List

Provide approximate quantities for one person for seven days:

- Vegetables.
- Fruits.
- Rice and millets.
- Pulses and legumes.
- Dairy products.
- Nuts and seeds.
- Other essential ingredients.

### E. Meal Preparation Guide

Explain:
- Which ingredients can be prepared in advance.
- How to prepare healthy breakfast options quickly.
- How to prepare lunch for office.
- How to manage dinner with minimal cooking.
- How to control oil and portion sizes.

### F. Foods to Prefer and Limit

Create two categories:

1. Foods to include regularly.
2. Foods to consume occasionally or limit.

Use familiar South Indian food examples.

### G. Weight-Loss Support Guidelines

Include:
- Daily hydration guidance.
- Walking and exercise recommendations.
- Strength-training recommendations.
- Sleep and recovery guidance.
- Ways to handle hunger and cravings.
- Weekly weight-monitoring recommendations.

### H. Final Practical Recommendations

Summarize the five most important habits to follow consistently for sustainable weight loss.

**IMPORTANT RESTRICTIONS:**
- Do not recommend crash diets or starvation.
- Do not eliminate rice completely.
- Do not recommend detox drinks or fat-burning drinks.
- Avoid unnecessary supplements.
- Prioritize affordable Tamil Nadu ingredients.
- Ensure adequate protein in every main meal.
- Do not assume all vegetarian foods are automatically high in protein.
- Clearly identify nutritional values as estimates.
- Consider medical conditions and recommend professional dietary guidance when necessary.

**FINAL OBJECTIVE:**

Deliver a realistic, affordable, nutritionally balanced 7-day South Indian vegetarian meal plan that an individual can follow consistently while losing weight safely, maintaining muscle, and enjoying traditional Tamil Nadu food.
